About Booth Buddy

Why Small Brands Should Switch to Booth Buddy

We organize our own pop-up events from start to finish—scouting great venues, promoting the bazaar, and preparing booth supplies so you can focus on selling. Whether you want a full booth to yourself or want to split the cost with a partner, we make popping up stress-free.

✕
The Old Way

Joining Traditional Bazaars

💸
High Solo Booth Rentals: Paying for a full space alone can be too heavy for small and starting brands.
😰
Risky Social Media Roommates: Searching for a booth-sharer in random groups often leads to last-minute backing out.
⚔️
Placed Beside Direct Competitors: Ending up right next to another merchant selling the exact same products.
🚚
Hassle Bringing Your Own Setup: Contacting separate outside suppliers just to rent tables, tents, and lights.

The Better Way

Popping Up With Booth Buddy

Easy Step-by-Step Process

How It Works

From signing up to meeting your Booth Buddy on event day—everything happens in four simple steps.

1

Create Your Profile

Fill out your brand details, choose your product category, and upload your basic business permit or ID for our team's review.

2

Pick an Event & Option

Choose from our upcoming pop-up events. Select a Solo Booth or pick Booth Buddy to split the space and cost.

3

Add Booth Supplies

Select any optional setup supplies you need—like a white canopy tent, folding table, or warm spotlights—and complete your booking.

4

Meet Your Buddy & Sell!

Once confirmed, open My Booking to see your matched Booth Buddy's brand profile, coordinate your setup, and enjoy the event!
